A bigger watch — Vakter's next chapter

Anti-theft stays free, forever. What's changing: Vakter is expanding from one kind of threat to all of them. Here's what that means for people who already own it.

What's changing

Vakter v1.4.4 — the version you bought — is an anti-theft alarm for your Mac. One shortcut to arm it. A 100 dB siren, a photo burst, ambient audio, and an iMessage to your phone the instant someone tries to walk off with it. That product is not going away. It will stay free, it will keep working, and every existing licence covers it.

What's changing is the scope of the watch.

Starting with v1.5 — due in the next few weeks — Vakter is becoming something wider: an on-device security companion that watches your Mac against the full range of threats most Mac owners actually encounter day-to-day. Mail scams. Suspicious iMessages. Malicious web pages that drop shell commands into your clipboard. Processes you don't recognise. Persistence implants that shouldn't be there.

No subscription. No renewal. No trial that converts automatically. €29 buys the Essential tier for the lifetime of the product — the same model as Bartender, CleanShot, or Hazel.

Why we're doing this

If you think about a Mac owner's actual list of security worries in ranked order, physical theft sits near the bottom — somewhere around seventh place. The top of the list is daily and mundane: phishing emails, fake tech-support popups, suspicious app installs, shell commands that appear in a clipboard after visiting a sketchy website, and the ambient unease of not knowing what's running on your own machine.

Vakter v1.4.4 answers concern number seven. The pivot answers the whole list — with the same architecture, the same privacy properties, and no cloud.

There's also a more honest admission embedded here. Most days, anti-theft software does nothing for you. A Mac stolen at a coffee shop is a real event, but not a daily one. The pivot changes Vakter from something that pays off once (if ever) to something that runs a quiet check every single day.

The brand contract — read this once, then trust it

Vakter never uses a cloud LLM. Every analysis — the Mail verdict, the Messages scan, the clipboard explanation, the process audit — runs on-device using Apple Foundation Models (macOS 15.1+). There is no server that receives your data, because there is no server.

Verify it yourself: disconnect from the internet. Analysis features still work. That's the test. That's the contract.

This is not a marketing claim about "privacy-first design." It's a technical architecture decision, and it's permanent. The watch stays on-device or it doesn't ship.

The reason is straightforward: the moment any of your Mail content, clipboard text, or process list touches a vendor's server — even in anonymised form — the trust model collapses. Norton, McAfee, and their successors all took that path. Vakter will not.

Pricing

If you own Vakter today, your licence covers the Free tier permanently. Anti-theft, the Defenses checklist, the on-device LLM explainer for those checks — all yours, no action required, no expiry.

The Essential tier — Mail Watch, Messages Watch, Web Watch, Mac Watch, and the chat panel — is a one-time €29 upgrade. There's a 7-day trial built in so you can verify it works before spending anything.

The upgrade flow will appear in the menubar when v1.5 ships. No pressure before that.

What's shipping when

  • v1.5 (next few weeks): Foundation Models integration, on-device Defenses explainer, LLM chat panel, pricing migration, 7-day Essential trial.
  • v1.6 (Q2–Q3 2026): Mail Watch — drag an email in, get a scam verdict in under two seconds. Sender-reputation baseline. URL safety checks. Public launch.
  • v1.7 (Q3 2026): Messages Watch — iMessage and SMS forwarded from iPhone, scam-pattern detection, inline "block + report."
  • v1.8 (Q3–Q4 2026): Web Watch (Safari extension) and Mac Watch — process audit, LaunchAgent review, code-signature analysis, persistence detection.

The full roadmap, with more detail on each release, is at vakter.app/roadmap.

What isn't changing

The anti-theft alarm. The siren. The photo burst. The ambient audio. The iMessage to your phone. The tamper-evident SHA-256 event chain. The Defenses checklist. The navy and amber. The lighthouse. The single shortcut: ⌃⌥⌘L. The no-cloud architecture. The no-telemetry stance. The no-accounts model. The €29 one-time pricing.

Vakter v1.4.4 is the watch it always was. v1.5 makes the watch wider. The night-watchman doesn't retire — the dock just gets longer.

A note to existing owners

If you bought Vakter as an anti-theft alarm and have no interest in the expanded scope, that's a reasonable position. The app you have will keep working exactly as it does today. Updates to the alarm itself, bug fixes, and macOS compatibility will remain free.

If you're curious about the Essential tier, the 7-day trial is there precisely so you can make the call with the actual software in front of you, not a description of it.
