AI features · v1.5

Your Mac, watched on your Mac.

Vakter v1.5 adds three on-device AI features that explain what your Mac is doing and why. Every one of them runs locally through Apple Foundation Models. Vakter never uses a cloud LLM. You can verify it yourself in airplane mode — the recipe is on this page.

The contract, in one line. Vakter never uses a cloud model. The Defenses explainer, the chat panel, the morning briefing — all of it runs on your Mac through Apple Foundation Models. No prompts leave the device. No responses arrive from a server. Disconnect from the internet and the AI features still work.

1. What the AI does

§ 01 / 04

Three features in v1.5. Each one calls Apple Foundation Models on the local device. The first is free; the other two unlock with the Essential one-time purchase.

Defenses explainer Free

Every item in the 20-point Defenses checklist gets a "Why does this matter?" button. Click it and a plain-English paragraph appears, tailored to your current macOS version. The explanation is generated locally by the on-device model the moment you ask — not pre-written, not cached on a server, not pulled from any network call.

Coming in v1.5

Chat panel Essential

"What's running on my Mac?" Ask Vakter anything about your system's security posture in a small chat panel. The model sees the same on-device context Vakter already has — your Defenses checklist results, your running modes, your event log summary — and answers from that, locally. Nothing you type leaves the device.

Coming in v1.5

Morning briefing Essential

A short summary of overnight events, generated when you wake your Mac. What armed, what disarmed, which Defenses checks shifted, whether anything would benefit from a closer look. Three to five sentences, on-device, before your first coffee.

Coming in v1.5

2. What it never does

§ 02 / 04

The contract is also a list of what Vakter will not do, in any version, at any tier, for any price. The constraint is the product.

  • Never sends a prompt to a cloud LLM. There is no OpenAI key in the bundle, no Anthropic endpoint, no Google Gemini call. The only model Vakter speaks to is the one Apple ships inside macOS.
  • Never uploads the contents of your disk. The chat panel reads what Vakter already knows about your Mac; it does not scan files into a remote index.
  • Never trains on your data. Apple Foundation Models run local inference only. Nothing you type into Vakter becomes a training signal for anyone.
  • Never phones home with telemetry. No usage events, no prompt logs, no anonymized analytics. The brand contract forbids the category, not just the obvious cases.
  • Never requires internet to function. Defenses explainer, chat panel, morning briefing — all three work with Wi-Fi off. That's the point of the airplane-mode demo below.
  • Never has a hidden bypass. No "premium support" mode that routes to a cloud model. No sales-enablement override. No law-enforcement backdoor. If we ever needed one, we would ship a different product under a different name.

3. Verify it yourself (airplane mode demo)

§ 03 / 04

You don't have to trust the claim. Four steps on your own Mac, sixty seconds, and you'll see the answer with your own eyes.

  1. Turn on airplane mode. Turn Wi-Fi off and Bluetooth off from Control Center. If you want to be thorough, unplug the Ethernet cable. The Mac is now a standalone device with no network path of any kind.
  2. Open Vakter, then Settings → Privacy → AI History. The AI History pane lists every prompt the model has run, with timestamps and the response it produced. The pane lives locally; it's a SQLite file in your Vakter Application Support folder.
  3. Click "Why does this matter?" on any Defenses check. Pick the easiest one — FileVault, Gatekeeper, Find My, whichever. The button calls Apple Foundation Models directly through the on-device API.
  4. The explainer renders. A paragraph appears, written for the check you clicked, in airplane mode. That paragraph could not have come from a server, because there is no network. It came from your Mac. The new row in AI History records the prompt and the response, both stored locally.

The same four-step demo works for the chat panel and the morning briefing once you've unlocked Essential. Same on-device path, same airplane-mode result.

4. Which Macs (system requirements)

§ 04 / 04

The AI features depend on Apple Foundation Models, which ships with macOS 15.1 and requires Apple Silicon for on-device inference. The anti-theft side of Vakter does not depend on the model and runs on a wider range of Macs.

  • macOS 15.1+ on Apple Silicon — full AI features. Defenses explainer, chat panel, and morning briefing all run through the on-device Apple Foundation Models API. M1 or later. Available on the day v1.5 ships.
  • macOS 14 — anti-theft only. Anti-theft stays free, forever, on macOS 14. The AI features simply don't appear in the menu — no gated paywall, no nag screen, no degraded experience. The Defenses checklist still works; it just doesn't get the "Why does this matter?" button.
  • Intel Macs — anti-theft only. Same story. Apple Foundation Models is an Apple Silicon API. Intel Macs get the watch, the alarm, the iMessage evidence, and the Defenses checklist as a free download. No AI features, no charge.
