- **Name** — Vakter ("the night-watchmen", Norwegian)
- **What** — macOS menubar anti-theft app
- **Who** — Jonathan Gebru, solo developer
- **Where** — Built in plain Swift on a Mac
- **When** — v1.4 currently in development (released YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Price** — $29 one-time. 14-day full trial. $12/yr optional for major-version updates
- **Distribution** — Direct DMG download from vakter.app (not Mac App Store; the privileged daemon cannot be sandboxed)
- **Requirements** — macOS 14.0+ (Sonoma), Apple Silicon or Intel, internal camera/mic/speakers

## Positioning, one paragraph

Find My is recovery. Vakter is the moment. Find My locates your Mac
after it's already gone — needs the device powered, signed-in, and
online. Vakter operates earlier: the instant the lid closes, the
cable pulls, or your trusted devices leave the room, Vakter screams
a siren, captures photos and audio, and texts your phone with a
Maps pin. They're complementary; we recommend running both.
