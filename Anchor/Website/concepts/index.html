Two-line header

The wordmark on line one, a humanized status sentence with a state-colored dot on line two. Bartender / Things 3 pattern. Reads at a glance.

Primary action up top

Disarm (or Arm) is the only thing in the amber tile. Keyboard ⌃⌥⌘L mirrors the global hotkey so muscle memory carries.

Mode + Diagnostics as submenus

The dropdown stays short. Five modes nest under Mode; testAlarm / runArmDemo / simulateLidClose nest under Diagnostics.

Bonus: Last event row

New in this concept. A two-line preview at the bottom of the menu — transition, evidence count, trigger, timestamp. One glance answers "did anything happen while I was away."

§ 06 · arming overlay

The handoff.

The full-screen moment that fires the instant Vakter takes the deck. Lives for ~0.6 s before the system lock takes over. Two variants: arming, and the calmer disarming acknowledgment.

§ 06 / 12

Borderless transparent NSWindow at .floating level, full-screen, ignoresMouseEvents = true. Fades in 0.18 s, holds 0.24 s, fades out 0.18 s. The screen-lock animation takes over before the user can interact — by design.

§ 07 · evidence delivery

Two channels. Same payload.

iMessage from your Apple ID to your own phone. Email from your Mail.app to anyone you trust. Both carry the photos, audio, and Maps URL. Both leave a paper trail in your sent folder.

Privacy & security vakter.app Changelog

Why this window matters

Every Mac utility has one. Most are perfunctory. Vakter's About window is a single moment where the brand voice gets to breathe: the etymology line, the named human, the unceremonious version string in monospace.

What's intentionally absent

No "Send feedback" button hiding a Typeform. No copyright wall. No legal notices stuffed below the fold. The About window is signed-by-the-developer, then it ends. Read SECURITY.md or the EULA if you want to.

Three actions, three destinations

Privacy & security opens the architecture page. vakter.app opens the marketing site. Changelog opens the release-notes page. Everything else is a setting.
